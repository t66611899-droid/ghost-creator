# Viral Strategy — Content Intelligence Rules

## Mission

Ghost Creator generates 30-day content empires for local businesses.
Every output must be engineered to stop a scroll within 2 seconds.

---

## The Virality Stack

### Hook Formula
```
[Pattern Interrupt] + [Curiosity Gap] + [Relatability Signal]
```
- Pattern Interrupt: unexpected visual, counterintuitive claim, or speed
- Curiosity Gap: leave ONE question unanswered in the first 3 words
- Relatability Signal: name the exact audience in the hook

### Day Progression Arc
| Days | Phase | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| 1–10 | Trust Building | Establish identity, show process, behind-the-scenes |
| 11–20 | Authority | Expert reveals, myth-busting, results showcase |
| 21–30 | Conversion | CTAs, social proof, limited offers, testimonials |

---

## Industry Hook Templates

### Barber
- Transformation reveals (before/after fades)
- "Watch me fix the worst haircut I've ever seen"
- Day-in-the-life: "5am at the shop"
- Client reactions: genuine surprise moments

### Gym / Fitness
- "The exercise everyone does wrong" → correction
- PR moments with crowd reaction
- "30-day transformation" time-lapses
- Nutrition myth-busting

### HVAC / Trades
- "You're wasting $X/month doing this" → fix reveal
- Diagnostic walk-throughs ("found this in a wall…")
- Seasonal urgency ("this will freeze in 2 weeks")
- Shocking before/after (dirty vs. clean unit)

### Real Estate
- Price reveal: "What $500K buys in [city] vs [city]"
- Secret listing walkthroughs
- Negotiation tip reveals
- Market prediction takes

---

## Script Structure (30–60 seconds)

```
0–2s:   HOOK — visual pattern interrupt + spoken hook
2–8s:   AMPLIFY — expand the curiosity gap
8–25s:  DELIVER — the actual value (teach/show/transform)
25–55s: PROOF — result, reaction, or transformation reveal
55–60s: CTA — one action only, spoken directly to camera
```

---

## Language Rules

- Hebrew scripts: use Rubik Bold, `dir="rtl"`, real Unicode (לא romanization)
- English scripts: ALL CAPS for emphasis words in director notes
- Bilingual: Hebrew first, English translation on new line
- Max 6 sentences per hook script — every word must earn its place

## Tone → Visual Style Map

| Tone | Camera Movement | Lighting | Edit Rhythm |
|------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Bold & Confident | Slow push-in | Dramatic side light | Hard cuts on beat |
| Friendly & Warm | Handheld, slight shake | Soft golden hour | Cross-dissolves |

| Hype & Energy | Whip pans, jump cuts | RGB accent lights | Sub-1s cuts |
| Luxury | Dolly, smooth slider | Backlit silhouette | Slow motion B-roll |
